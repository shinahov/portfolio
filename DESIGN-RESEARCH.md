# Design research and decisions — 6 September 2026

## Sources read

- Community discussion: https://www.reddit.com/r/vibecoding/comments/1ta6r5y/why_do_all_aigenerated_websites_look_exactly_the/
- Community critique of proposed fixes: https://www.reddit.com/r/cursor/comments/1vvznhr/i_finally_figured_out_why_every_aicoded_site/

These are qualitative opinions, not representative survey results or reliable authorship detection. Promotional posts repeating the same purple-gradient advice were not treated as independent evidence. A font or color cannot establish whether a website was made with AI.

## Recurring criticism and the response here

1. Interchangeable hero / cards / CTA page structure. Replace the huge hero with a compact personal introduction beside the work. Make the first three projects concrete exhibits, followed by a readable project index.
2. Repeated equal cards, pills, glass, glows, and gradients. Use open rows, restrained rules, text links, one native category selector, and no decorative effects.
3. Generic slogans and unsupported claims. Use the owner's actual role, university, compiler example, screenshots, project scope, and dates. Remove “Software & curiosity,” “Explore my work,” and decorative numbered section labels.
4. Palette swaps presented as redesigns. Replace both old stylesheets with one new stylesheet and change the complete page structure, navigation, project renderer, typography, and lower-page layout.
5. Cookie-cutter solutions to cookie-cutter design. Avoid replacing one universal template with another. The main exhibits reflect this owner's compiler, route simulation, and formal clustering work; no invented testimonials, statistics, charts, or awards.

## Chosen direction

Personal software portfolio with a compact profile column, forest-green introduction, white work surface, burnt-orange links, Trebuchet headings, Verdana body text, and monospace only for actual source code. Real project images are shown as screenshots rather than cropped into decorative circles. The user's original screenshot informs the breathing room and restraint; the latest request supersedes its exact colors and circular composition.

All existing project details, videos, source links, contact methods, light/dark switching, and education/experience remain available. Responsive layouts stack naturally. This is a local redesign with no remote push or publication.
